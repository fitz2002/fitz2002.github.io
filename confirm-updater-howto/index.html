What this tool does

One form, every vendor email, one click

Every trip has a confirmation template — a Word document your Trip Expert (TE) builds, with one section per vendor (hotels, restaurants, activity providers) and short codes like [D1_DATE] or [NUM_GUESTS] standing in for real information. Normally, prep day means opening that document and manually swapping every code for the real value, in every section, for every vendor, before copying each email into Outlook by hand.

The Email Updater does that swap for you. You upload the template once, fill in the trip's details in a short form, and the tool builds every vendor email automatically — then sends all of them through your own Outlook account with one click.

  • You don't edit the template. The codes and vendor sections are already set up by your TE — you just fill in a form.
  • You always get to preview first. Every email is shown to you before anything sends, and you can edit or skip individual ones.
  • It sends as you, through your own Microsoft/Outlook sign-in — recipients see it come from your real email address, and a copy lands in your Sent Items, same as if you'd typed it yourself.
Before you start

Have these ready

Gather these first so you're not hunting for information halfway through the form.

  • The trip's confirmation template — the .docx file from your TE (the "Standardized Prep Day Confirmation Template"). A .txt file works too, if that's what you were given.
  • Your Backroads Microsoft 365 login — the same one you use for Outlook.
  • The guest backgrounder — for dietary restrictions and any special room requests.
  • Guest and leader counts — total guests, adults, kids and their ages, number of leaders, and leader phone numbers.
  • Hotel room counts — how many rooms are booked at each property.
  • Pop-ups allowed for this page — signing in opens a small Microsoft window, and a pop-up blocker will silently stop it.
Step 1

Upload the email template

1

Upload Email Template

Click the upload box (or drag the file onto it) and choose the template your TE gave you. Once it loads, a preview of the raw text appears underneath — that's just to confirm the right file loaded; you don't need to read through it.

You won't need to touch the vendor sections, the TO: / Subject: / EMAIL BODY: labels, or any of the bracketed codes inside the document. The form in the rest of this guide fills those in for you.

Step 2

Trip details

2

Trip Details

Number of Trip Days — how many days this template covers (check the Vendor Checklist in the document for the highest "Day" listed, e.g. D1–D6).

Prep Day Date — today's date, the day you're doing this prep work (Day 0). The guests' first day, D1, is automatically calculated as the day after it, and every later day fills in underneath from there — you never need to calculate or type those by hand.

Prep Staff Name — your name. It signs off every email the tool generates.

Step 3

Guest details

3

Guest Details

Number of Guests — the total headcount for the trip. This field is required; you won't be able to send until it's filled in.

Dietary Restrictions — one guest per line, pulled from the guest backgrounder, e.g.:

Karen: Gluten-free
Dennis: Nut allergy
Helen: Vegetarian

If there are none, leave it blank — the emails will say so automatically instead of showing an empty line.

☐ Kids Trip?

Check Kids Trip? only if kids are coming along. Checking it reveals three more fields right underneath: Number of Adults, Number of Kids, and Kids Ages — fill those in once they appear.

All-adult trip? Leave it unchecked The checkbox is off by default. For a trip with no one under 18, skip it — the Adults, Kids, and Kids Ages fields stay hidden and you don't need to touch them.
Step 4

Leader details

4

Leader Details

Set Number of Leaders and a Name + phone number field appears for each one — fill those in.

Co-leader emails — every vendor email's CC line, semicolon-separated. Anything the template already CCs is combined with this list automatically, so you won't get duplicates.

Step 5

Hotel details

5

Hotel Details

Set Number of Hotels and a room-count field appears for each one, in the same order as the vendor checklist in your template.

Special Requests for Hotels — anything from the guest backgrounder about rooming together, accessibility, or upgrades.

Step 6

Sign in with Microsoft

6

Microsoft Outlook Authentication

This is the last thing you do before sending — it's how the tool is allowed to send email as you. Once the rest of the form looks right, click the button near the bottom of the page:

🔑 Sign in with Microsoft Not authenticated

A small Microsoft window pops up. Choose your Backroads account, sign in as you normally would, and the window closes itself. The status pill on the page changes to confirm it worked:

Authenticated
Why this comes last Everything above (Steps 1–5) is safe to fill in, fix, and re-check freely — nothing sends yet. This step is deliberately placed right before Review & Send, because signing in is the last thing standing between you and actually emailing vendors. See "Preview before you sign in" in Step 7.
Step 7

Review & send

7

Review & Send

When every field above is filled in, click:

✉️ Process & Send All Emails
Preview before you sign in As long as you haven't completed Step 6 (Sign in with Microsoft) yet, this button only builds a preview — it shows you every email exactly as it would be sent, but sends nothing. Use this to catch mistakes safely. Once you are signed in, the same button sends immediately — there's no separate "are you sure" step, so only sign in once the preview looks right.

Each vendor becomes its own card, showing who it's going to, the subject line, and the full message. Two buttons sit on every card:

  • ✏️ Edit — opens the subject, recipients, CC, and body so you can fix anything by hand, then Save.
  • 🗑 Delete — marks that one email as skipped (with an Undo, in case you change your mind). Use this for a vendor that doesn't need an email this trip.
Red warning on a card A red line reading "Unresolved placeholder(s)" means a code like [D2_DATE] is still showing instead of a real value — almost always because a field back in Steps 2–5 was left blank, or there's a typo in the template itself. That email is automatically held back and will not send until it's fixed. Go back and fill in the missing field, then click Process & Send again — or use Edit to fix the text directly.
Clicking the button again resets the preview Each click rebuilds all the cards from scratch, using whatever is currently in the form. Any per-email Edits or Deletes you made get wiped out. So make your edits right before you're ready to actually send — not several clicks earlier.

Once sending starts, each card's status updates as it goes:

Pending Sent Failed / Blocked Removed

A running Send Log underneath narrates everything as it happens — how many emails were found, which were skipped and why, and a final tally of how many sent successfully. A copy of every sent email also lands in your own Outlook Sent Items, exactly like sending it yourself.

Attachments aren't automatic If a vendor section mentions an attachment (a rooming list, for example), the tool does not attach it — it only sends the text of the email. Check each template's notes for "Attach rooming list" and send those separately in Outlook, or add them after opening the message from your Sent Items.
Troubleshooting

If something looks wrong

A card is red and says "Unresolved placeholder(s)"

A bracketed code didn't get replaced. Check the matching field in Steps 2–5 is filled in — the warning names the exact code, e.g. [D3_DATE] points back to Trip Details, [LEADER_2_PHONE] to Leader Details. If the field is filled in and it's still unresolved, the template likely has a typo — flag it to your TE.

A card says "No To: recipients found"

The vendor's section in the template is missing a valid TO: line, or the addresses aren't formatted as real emails. Use Edit to type the correct address in directly, or fix it in the source document and re-upload.

The Microsoft sign-in window won't finish, or nothing happens when I click it

Check your browser isn't blocking the pop-up — look for a blocked-popup icon in the address bar and allow it for this page, then try again. Make sure you're picking your real Backroads account in the picker, not a personal one.

Some emails failed partway through with an authentication error

Your sign-in can time out on a long prep session. Note which vendors show "Failed" or "Sent" in the Send Log before doing anything else. Go back to Step 6 and sign in again — but re-clicking Process & Send All Emails rebuilds every card from scratch and will try to resend the ones that already succeeded too. Before clicking it again, click Delete on any card the log already marked "Sent" so vendors don't get a duplicate email, then send the rest.

I uploaded the file but nothing shows up

Only .docx and .txt files are supported — an older .doc file needs to be re-saved as .docx in Word first (File → Save As → Word Document).

Quick answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to edit the Word document at all?

No. Your TE sets up the vendor sections and placeholder codes when the template is built. Your job is just filling out the form on this page — the tool handles every substitution.

Who do the emails come from?

You. Sending happens through your own signed-in Outlook account, so vendors see your real name and address, and replies land in your normal inbox.

Can I stop a vendor from getting an email without editing the template?

Yes — click Delete on that vendor's card during the preview, before you send. It's marked Removed and skipped; Undo brings it back if needed.

What happens to the emails after they're sent?

Each one is saved to your Outlook Sent Items automatically, just as if you'd sent it by hand — that's your record for the trip.

Is it safe to click Process & Send All Emails more than once before I've signed in?

Yes — while you're not signed in, it only ever previews and never sends. Click it as many times as you need while you're still fixing fields.
